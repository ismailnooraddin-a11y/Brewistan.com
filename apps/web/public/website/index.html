A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Simple by design.
Clear from the start.

Here's what to know before your first stamp.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Sign up as a cafe owner, register your cafe and first branch, and submit the requested business verification. Your 14-day trial begins after approval, not while you wait for review. New cafes start on Classic. Your dashboard shows paid options and current pricing.

Who can sign up through this website?

This website's signup is for cafe owners. Customers join by scanning a participating cafe's QR. Baristas receive access from their cafe owner; there is no public barista signup here. Using an owner signup link does not bypass business verification or approval.

Do customers need to download an app?

No app-store download is required for the web experience. Customers can use the browser or add Brewistan to their home screen as a PWA. An external QR may open in a browser rather than the installed app, depending on the phone. Open Brewistan directly or use its in-app cafe scanner for a consistent return visit.

Does one card work at all my branches?

Yes. A customer has one card per cafe, with shared progress across that cafe's branches. Each branch has its own entry QR. That QR opens the cafe journey; it does not award a stamp. An authorized barista scans the customer's QR and confirms the action.

Where do I change my card and rewards?

Open Branding in your owner dashboard for the card design options included in your package. Open Campaign to set your stamp goal, reward and available rules. Signature and Bespoke have additional branding options; Classic keeps a polished standard design.

Are Apple Wallet, Google Wallet and plastic cards ready?

The current product includes the Brewistan web-app card wallet. Native Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes, and physical plastic-card support, are planned integrations and are not included today. The diagram marks them with dashed lines so the distinction is clear.

Can I use email instead of my phone number?

First-time cafe registration currently starts with phone verification. Once an email is linked and verified on your account, it can be used for email OTP sign-in when delivery is configured. There is no password to remember.
